# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mac users, developers, and AI-assisted builders who live in a terminal and value immediate startup, very low resource use, keyboard control, and an environment they can reshape without replacing its native core.

## Product Purpose

Termatica is a tiny native macOS terminal and extensible AI harness. Its universal release app targets 1 MB or less while providing a real interactive shell, reliable copy/paste, portable themes, and installable tools stored outside the bundle.

Success means the terminal launches almost instantly, presents only the shell by default, remains useful without extensions, and becomes substantially more capable through a language-neutral extension protocol.

## Brand Personality

Raw, fast, and terminal-native. Termatica starts as a restrained neutral terminal with a complete ANSI palette. Optional phosphor themes add pixel-minded monospace typography, glow, and CRT texture. The app icon is a single white lightning bolt with no frame or decorative container.

## Anti-references

Avoid Electron chrome, IDE density, dashboards, permanent toolbars, horizontal tab bars, oversized controls, rounded web cards, candy gradients, and generic neon cyberpunk decoration. The tiny numbered tab rail appears only when more than one shell exists; the default window must never compete with the shell.

## Design Principles

1. The shell is the whole surface by default.
2. Native speed and a sub-1 MB core are product features.
3. Retro effects must preserve legibility and can always be disabled.
4. Complexity belongs in optional extensions and user-owned files.
5. Every color, font, opacity, blur, glow, cursor, and texture value is inspectable and portable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide keyboard operation, standard macOS copy/paste conventions, visible focus, high-contrast themes, scalable text, configurable effects, and VoiceOver-readable terminal state. Respect users who disable transparency by allowing fully opaque themes.
